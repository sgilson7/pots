# Glossary

The source of truth for what each word in this game means. Strings, tests, the plan and the README use a term in the sense given here. The page shows a definition when a reader hovers the term.

Each entry is a `### Term` heading, one or two plain sentences, and an optional `Not:` line of words that must not be used for it. Entries are in alphabetical order. `crates/core/tests/copy.rs` checks the format and the `Not:` words; `TONE.md` governs the prose.

### Agility

An attribute. Each point adds armor and attack speed, and an agility hero's damage.

### All Random All Mid

A mode of the custom match and online play. Each player is dealt a random hero, and all ten fight in the middle lane. Heroes start a few levels up and earn experience and gold faster than in a normal match, so it plays shorter.

### Armor

Cuts the physical damage a unit takes. Each point of armor counts for less than the one before, so no amount makes a unit immune. Negative armor makes it take more.

Not: defence, defense

### Attribute bonus

A fifth choice for a level's point: a little strength, agility and intelligence at once.

### Barracks

A building behind each lane's last tower. While it stands, the enemy's creeps in that lane stay ordinary; when it falls, your side's creeps there grow stronger.

### Buyback

Paying gold to return from death at once, instead of waiting to respawn. It then waits a while before it can be used again.

### Cannon

Stands beside each side's fountain in All Random All Mid. From the fountain it throws your hero onto the middle lane, no farther than just past your outer tower, and it loads again each time you return.

### Cooldown

The time an ability or item needs before it can be used again.

### Courier

Your side's pack animal. It carries what you buy from the base to your hero.

### Creep

A unit that walks a lane on its own. A new wave leaves each base every half minute.

Not: minion

### Damage

What an attack takes from a unit's health before armor. The white number is the hero's own; the bonus after it comes from items and effects.

### Deny

Killing one of your own side's units below half health so the enemy gains no gold and less experience from it.

### Glyph

Makes every tower of your side shrug off attacks for a few seconds. Your side shares it, and it waits minutes between uses.

### Gold

What you buy items with. Most of it comes from last hits; killing heroes and taking towers pays too. Dying loses some of it.

### Health relic

A flask that waits in the middle lane in All Random All Mid. Walk over it to get back some health and mana; it returns a while after it is taken.

### Intelligence

An attribute. Each point adds mana and mana regeneration, and an intelligence hero's damage.

### Lane

One of the three roads between the two bases: the top lane, the middle lane and the bottom lane.

### Last hit

The killing blow on a unit. Only the hero who lands it earns its gold.

### Magic resistance

Cuts the magic damage a unit takes. Heroes resist part of all magic damage.

### Mana

What abilities cost to use. It refills slowly over time and quickly at your fountain.

### Movement speed

How fast a unit walks, in map units a second.

### Neutral camp

A group of creeps that belongs to no side, waiting in the forest. A camp refills on the minute if no one stands in it.

### Reroll

In All Random All Mid, trading the hero you were dealt for another from your side's deck before the match starts.

### Respawn

A hero who dies returns at the fountain after a wait that grows with the hero's level.

### Rune

A charm that appears in the river every second minute. The hero who walks over it gains its power for a while.

### Slow

Lowers a unit's movement speed or attack speed, or both, for a while.

### Sphere

The great orb at the heart of each base. The side whose Sphere breaks loses the match.

Not: ancient, throne, nexus

### Strength

An attribute. Each point adds health and health regeneration, and a strength hero's damage.

### Stun

Stops a unit from moving, attacking or using anything for a while.

### Tower

A building that guards a lane. Towers fall in order: each one can be hurt only after the one before it in its lane has fallen.

Not: turret

### Ultimate

A hero's fourth ability. It opens at levels six, eleven and sixteen.
